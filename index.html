Sample 1: Performance Marketing Copy

Concept: a payments app for small Nigerian businesses. Written for Meta and Google.

Meta advert, main text: Your customers already pay with transfers. The problem is confirming 20 of them before market closes. With one business account, every payment lands in one place, and you see it instantly. No more screenshots. No more asking, have you seen it?

Headline: Every payment. One account. Seen instantly. Action button: Open a free business account

Google advert: Business Account for Traders and Shops. Receive Payments Instantly. No Hidden Charges. Open a business account in minutes. Accept transfers and card payments, track every naira, and pay suppliers from one simple app.

TikTok hook, first 3 seconds: Stop screenshotting transfer receipts. There is a better way.

Why this works: it names a real daily pain, confirming transfers, answers it in one line, and keeps the language the way a trader actually speaks.

Sample 2: CRM Email Copy

Concept: welcome email for a new business account holder.

Subject: Welcome. Your first payment is easier than you think

Hello [First name], Welcome aboard. You did the hard part. You opened the account. Now let us make your first week simple. One, add your business name so customers see the right name when they pay you. Two, share your account details on your shop page, status, or receipt. Three, watch your first payment land. You will get an instant alert, every time. If anything feels confusing, reply to this email. A real person will answer you. Let us grow your business, one payment at a time.

Why this works: one email, one job. Finish setup. Short sentences, numbered steps, and a reply invitation that builds trust.

Sample 3: App and Product Copy

Concept: in app messages for a banking app. Goal: make finance feel simple.

Empty state: Nothing here yet, and that is fine. When you receive or send money, it will show up here instantly.

Payment success: Payment sent. Your money is on its way to [name]. You will both get a confirmation.

Error message: That did not go through, and your money has not moved. Please check the account number and try again.

Onboarding tip: This is your balance. Tap it any time to see exactly how your money moved today.

Why this works: no banking jargon. Every message answers the one question the user has at that moment: what happened, and is my money safe?

Sample 4: Social Media Campaign

Concept: a one week campaign, Made for Hustle, celebrating small business owners.

Launch post: Behind every big business is a person who refused to stop. This week, we celebrate the shop owners, traders, and creators building something real, one customer at a time. You are Made for Hustle.

Education carousel: Three numbers every shop owner should check every Friday. What came in. What went out. What is left. That last one is your real profit, not just your sales. Check them every week. Small leaks sink big shops.

Customer story post: I used to write payments in a notebook. Now my phone remembers everything. Every trader, once they switch.

Why this works: it respects the audience. It teaches something useful, speaks their language, and sells the feeling of control, not just the product.

Sample 5: Script for Motion and Animated Video, 30 seconds

Concept: animated explainer for receiving business payments.

Scene 1, a busy shop, phone buzzing. Voiceover: Another transfer? Let me check, let me check. Scene 2, the shop owner smiles at one clean alert. Voiceover: Or just one alert. Payment received. Done. Scene 3, the sales for the day adding up on screen. Voiceover: Every payment, in one place. Every naira, accounted for. End card. Voiceover: Spend less time checking. Spend more time selling. On screen text: Open your business account today.

Why this works: the script shows the before and the after in the world of the customer, in under 30 seconds, with one clear action at the end.
